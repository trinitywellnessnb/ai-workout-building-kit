# Trainer Personalization Questionnaire

Complete this questionnaire after installing the package. ChatGPT can then transfer the answers into the configuration files.

## Business identity

1. What name should appear in trainer-facing and client-facing material?
2. What is the business name?
3. Which professional credentials should be displayed?
4. What email, phone number, website, and booking link may be used?
5. Which social-media profiles may be included?
6. What timezone and service area should the system assume?

## Brand voice

1. How should the business sound in client messages?
2. Which words, phrases, or claims should be preferred?
3. Which words, phrases, or claims should be avoided?
4. Should messages use the trainer's name, the business name, or first-person language?
5. Upload or identify the logo and preferred brand colors, if any.

## Training services

1. Who is the primary client population?
2. Which specialties and services are offered?
3. Where do clients normally train?
4. Which equipment is normally available?
5. What workout durations and program lengths are most common?

## Programming preferences

1. Use the recommended workout naming format, preserve the existing system, or create a hybrid?
2. Are warm-ups and cooldowns required by default?
3. How should RPE, tempo, supersets, circuits, and rest intervals normally be used?
4. What should happen when a requested movement is not in the verified Movement Registry?
5. Should existing verified workouts be reused before creating variations?
6. Which changes always require trainer approval?

## Calendar and engagement

1. Which check-ins, reminders, education messages, and end-of-program messages are normally used?
2. Which contact, booking, product, affiliate, or social links may be used?
3. What is the maximum acceptable message frequency?
4. Which claims, promotions, or messages require review before scheduling?

## Calibration

1. Describe one representative workout the system should build as its first test.
2. Describe what would make the test clearly wrong.
3. Should verified trainer corrections be recorded as reusable future guidance?
